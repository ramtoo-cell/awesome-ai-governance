# Contributing to Awesome AI Governance

Thanks for helping keep this list sharp. Suggestions are welcome — here's how to propose a resource.

## How to suggest a resource

1. **Open a pull request** adding your entry to the right section, keeping alphabetical-ish grouping where it already exists.
2. Use this exact format:
   `- [Name](https://link-to-resource) — One crisp sentence on what it is and why it belongs here.`
3. One resource per line. Keep descriptions to a single sentence.

## What gets accepted

- **Real and verifiable** — the project, paper, or standard must actually exist with a working link.
- **Maintained or canonical** — actively maintained tools, or canonical standards/papers that don't need maintenance.
- **Relevant** — it must help someone *govern, assure, evaluate, red-team, or audit* AI systems or agents. General ML libraries don't belong here.
- **Open where possible** — open-source tools and open standards are preferred; commercial products are accepted only if they publish genuinely useful open methodology.

## What gets rejected

- Dead links, vaporware, or resources you can't verify.
- Anything that requires naming an employer or leaking private information.
- Spam, self-promotion with no substance, or affiliate links.

Small fixes (typos, dead links, better descriptions) are always welcome — no need to ask first.
